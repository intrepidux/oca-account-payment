Cancel a posted payment and reopen the standard Register Payment wizard
prefilled with the original payment data, keeping full traceability between
the cancelled payment and the new one.

Usage
=====

#. Open a **posted** customer or vendor payment that is reconciled with
   invoice(s) / bill(s) and **not** matched with a bank statement.

   Or open the **paid/partial invoice** itself.
#. Click **Replace Payment** (form header) or use *Action → Replace Payment*.
#. The payment is cancelled (after automatic unreconcile) and the standard
   **Register Payment** wizard opens, prefilled with journal, date, amount,
   method, memo and simple write-off when applicable.
#. Adjust only what is wrong and confirm.
#. Both payments stay linked:

   * original → *Replacement Payment*
   * new → *Replaced Payment*

   Chatter messages document the replacement on both records.

If the invoice has several payments, the action opens the payment list so you
can open one and replace it.
